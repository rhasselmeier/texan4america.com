Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what Texan4America ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use this website. Texan4America is a blog and podcast. This page describes only what the site actually does today.

What we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send through the launch form on the home page, plus what is needed to deliver that form and keep spam out:

  • Information you submit. The form asks for your name, email address, and an optional note about topics you would like covered. We use that information to notify you when the blog and podcast launch and to respond to your note.
  • Bot check. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile so we can tell a person from a bot. Cloudflare receives what it needs to run that check and may set cookies or similar storage for verification.
  • Form delivery. When you submit the form, your entries are sent to a Twilio-hosted function so we can receive the message.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of visits. We do not run our own analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking scripts on this site.

The form does not ask for a phone number, payment details, or an account password. There is no separate newsletter service. There is no comment system.

Third-party embeds and players

This site does not currently embed a podcast player, video player, comment widget, social feed, or other third-party content on our pages. If we add embeds later, we will update this policy.

Comments and submissions

There are no public comments. The only submission on the site is the launch-notification form described above.

How we use and share information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the providers that make the site and form work (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Turnstile, and the Twilio function that receives form submissions), and only as needed for those purposes, or if the law requires it.

Cookies

We do not set our own cookies. Cloudflare Turnstile may set cookies or use local storage to complete the bot check.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do.

Contact
